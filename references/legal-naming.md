# Legal naming rules

## Required pattern

Prefer this Russian filename structure:

`[Вид документа] [№ при наличии] [краткий предмет] от [дата] на [число] л.[расширение]`

Examples:

- `Договор № 00-002102 на услуги по обращению с ТКО от 01.03.2019 на 6 л.pdf`
- `Акт выездной проверки здания по ул. Садовой, 27 от 24.09.2021 на 4 л.pdf`
- `Письмо о направлении справки о балансовой стоимости без даты на 1 л.pdf`

## Decision rules

- Start with the true document type: договор, контракт, соглашение, письмо, заявка, приказ, постановление, акт, справка, выписка, ходатайство, screenshot, or a precise neutral equivalent.
- Retain legally identifying numbers, dates, subject matter, periods, parties, addresses, or case numbers only when needed to distinguish the file.
- Shorten long official headings without losing legal meaning.
- Use the document's content rather than trusting a transliterated or technical filename. Record material corrections in the final audit.
- If the date is absent or not reliably legible, use `без даты`. Do not use a guessed creation, scan, download, or message date.
- Omit a missing number; do not add `б/н` unless it is part of the document or needed to distinguish it.
- For an agreement whose own date is missing, distinguish the agreement's date from the base contract's date: `Дополнительное соглашение № 2 к контракту № 2034-С от 19.02.2019 без даты ...`.
- Put the actual leaf count at the end: `на 1 л.`, `на 2 л.`, `на 15 л.`. For one-image files, use one leaf when the task requires this convention.
- Avoid vague names such as `Документ`, `Скан`, `Приложение`, or a bare `Договор` when the content supports a better name.
- Use one consistent style: first word capitalized; ordinary words lower-case; preserve abbreviations, proper names, and official case numbers.
- Replace slashes in document numbers with safe filename separators such as hyphens. Do not alter the number's meaning.
- Preserve the source extension unless conversion is requested.

## Ambiguity

Use a cautious but useful name when content is unclear, for example:

- `Нечитаемая копия договора без даты на 3 л.pdf`
- `Документ о расчетах без даты на 2 л.pdf`

Maintain a short ambiguity list containing the old filename, chosen neutral wording, and the reason certainty was not possible.

## Independent documents versus image sets

Do not combine already separate, independently identifiable documents merely because they share a folder. Combine images or mixed scan fragments only when the task-specific instructions or the user identify them as one set. Preserve all source pages and the explicitly requested order.
